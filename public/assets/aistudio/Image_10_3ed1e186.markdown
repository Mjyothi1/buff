## Brand & Style

This design system channels an unapologetic collision of Y2K pop nostalgia, modern neo-brutalism, and dopamine-inducing maximalism. Created for high-protein artisan indulgence, the visual environment directly contradicts the sterile, medicinal tone of traditional fitness supplements and the muted craft aesthetic of artisanal creameries. 

The emotional target is pure kinetic joy: uninhibited indulgence meets athletic ambition. Bold, hyper-saturated contrast drives the interface, combining chunky structural frameworks with playful, sticker-covered editorial pacing. Tactile neo-brutalism anchors the layout via stark black keylines and hard offset shadows, while vibrant pops of chartreuse, bubblegum, and cobalt spark high-energy interaction. The aesthetic screams athletic grit coated in hyper-sweet candy gloss.

## Colors

The palette operates on high-voltage tension:
- **Primary (`#D4FF00` - Neon Chartreuse):** Used for marquee accents, primary CTA backgrounds, interactive highlights, and high-impact metric badges.
- **Secondary (`#FF2E93` - Electric Bubblegum):** Drives secondary actions, promotional stickers, taste callouts, and expressive hover states.
- **Tertiary (`#2E4CFF` - Ultra Cobalt):** Provides punchy brand contrast, anchoring nutritional data tables, primary links, and structural borders when hyper-saturation is required.
- **Neutral Base (`#121212` - Licorice Charcoal):** The bedrock of typography, chunky borders, and offset hard drop shadows.
- **Canvas (`#FAF8F5` - Cream Milk):** Serves as the primary surface background to maintain high-contrast legibility and balance the neon intensity with an authentic dairy-inspired warmth.

Use color unsparingly in solid, non-gradient blocks to enforce graphic clarity and physical print-era pop.

## Typography

The type system blends expressive hyper-bold geometry with technical precision:
- **Display & Headlines (`Syne`):** Extra-wide, sculptural, and unapologetic. Large headlines should maintain tight tracking to command the page like a poster headline.
- **Body Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Warm, geometric, and highly readable. Prevents visual fatigue across ingredient lists, product descriptions, and functional e-commerce flows.
- **Stickers & Nutritional Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Monospaced-flavored tech sans rendered in all-caps. Used for nutrition callouts (`25G PROTEIN`, `0G SUGAR`), functional UI controls, pricing, and tabular data.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid desktop framework that condenses to 6 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile:
- **Rhythm:** Dense, high-energy block compositions. Content modules alternate between full-width color-blocked bands and staggered multi-column card grids.
- **Safe Margins:** Deep `3rem` margins on desktop to frame the brutalist content boundary, collapsing dynamically to `1.25rem` on mobile to maximize viewport density.
- **Spacing Principle:** Compact internal paddings combined with intentional asymmetric element offsets create the feeling of overlapping product packaging and physical stickers.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids soft, atmospheric blurs or diffuse gradients. Elevation is achieved exclusively through **hard neo-brutalist projection**:
- **Baseline Borders:** A uniform `2.5px` solid Licorice (`#121212`) border defines cards, buttons, badges, and modals.
- **Hard Offset Shadows:** Interactive and floating elements project a rigid `4px 4px 0px 0px #121212` shadow.
- **Elevation Scaling:**
  - *Resting Card / Badge:* `3px 3px 0px 0px #121212`
  - *Resting Button / Drawer:* `4px 4px 0px 0px #121212`
  - *Hover State:* Translate `-2px, -2px` with a deepened shadow of `6px 6px 0px 0px #121212`
  - *Active / Pressed State:* Translate `+3px, +3px` with shadow reduced to `0px 0px 0px 0px #121212`
- **Sticker Overlays:** Graphics and badges utilize non-conformist rotations (`-3deg` to `4deg`) to float above base content grids.

## Shapes

The geometric identity balances hard brutalist construction with plump, soft confectionery roundness:
- **Default Corners:** Core surfaces, product containers, and dialogue frames utilize standard `0.5rem` (`roundedness: 2`) corner radii.
- **Pill Exceptions:** High-protein stickers, nutrient badges, chips, and primary CTAs strictly deploy complete pill geometry (`9999px`) to create high visual contrast against boxy container layouts.
- **Wavy Divider Accents:** Section transitions occasionally adopt scalloped or dripping vector outlines to evoke melting ice cream while retaining crisp stroke definitions.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Neon Chartreuse (`#D4FF00`) fill, `2.5px` Licorice (`#121212`) stroke, `4px 4px 0px #121212` offset shadow, pill-shaped (`9999px`), Space Grotesk 700 uppercase. On hover: shifts `-2px, -2px` with `6px 6px` shadow. Active: snaps flat with `0px` shadow.
- **Secondary:** Electric Bubblegum (`#FF2E93`) fill or Ultra Cobalt (`#2E4CFF`) fill with milk-white text, matching the border and shadow behavior.
- **Ghost / Outline:** Transparent fill, `2.5px` Licorice border, resting `3px 3px 0px #121212` shadow.

### Sticker Badges & Chips
- Fully rounded pills displaying vital macro indicators (`25G PROTEIN`, `0G SUGAR`, `KETO-FRIENDLY`).
- Alternates between high-voltage fills: Chartreuse with black text, Bubblegum with white text, or pure White with black text.
- Pinned at jaunty angles (`transform: rotate(-2.5deg)`) over card headers or hero photography.

### Cards
- Milk White (`#FAF8F5`) or high-contrast tinted surface containers.
- Enclosed in `2.5px` Licorice borders with a resting `4px 4px 0px #121212` hard shadow.
- Padded with `1.5rem` (`space-lg`), pairing high-resolution scoop photography with bold nutrient metric callout grids.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Crisp Milk White containers with `2.5px` Licorice border and `0.5rem` corner radius.
- Inactive placeholder set in subdued charcoal; focused state introduces a hard `3px 3px 0px #D4FF00` or `#2E4CFF` neon shadow projection.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Chunky `20px` squares (checkboxes) and circles (radios) with `2.5px` border.
- Checked state fills with Ultra Cobalt (`#2E4CFF`) or Electric Bubblegum (`#FF2E93`) featuring an extra-thick, sharp black or white mark.

### Nutrition Marquee / Ticker
- Continuous, high-velocity horizontal strip set with Chartreuse background, solid borders, and bold Syne/Space Grotesk typography repeating punchy statements (`HIGH PROTEIN • ZERO GUILT • PURE FUNK`).
